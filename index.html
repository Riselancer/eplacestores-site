Questions

Before you ask.

Who owns the store?
You do. The eBay account stays in your name, and you can see everything we do at any time.
Is my account safe with you?
Account health is the first thing we protect. We follow eBay's policies, keep risky brand names out of listings, and fix warnings quickly. We'll explain exactly how we access and work on your store before we start.
How is profit calculated?
Sales minus product cost, shipping, eBay fees and ad spend. We send the numbers every month, and the remaining profit is split 50/50.
What if my store makes no profit in a month?
Then there is no profit to split, so you pay nothing for that month. The only fixed payment is the $250 setup.
Do you work with new stores?
Yes, but we're the best fit for sellers who are already selling and want to grow, or who are stuck with account problems.
Which marketplaces do you cover?
Mainly eBay US. We also manage Amazon, Walmart and Temu stores.
