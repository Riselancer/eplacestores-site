FAQ

Questions sellers ask before starting

What does the $200 cover?

A full review of your store, setting up sourcing and the tools we use, and the first round of product research and listings. It's paid once, at the start.

How do you calculate profit?

Sales, minus product cost, eBay and payment fees, shipping, ad spend and returns. You get every number in a monthly report before anything is split.

Does the store stay in my name?

Yes. The eBay account and the money in it stay yours. We work on the store as your managers.

Do you only work with eBay?

eBay US is our main focus. We also manage Amazon, Walmart and Temu stores for sellers who already sell there.

What do you need from me to start?

Your store link for the audit. If you decide to go ahead, access to the store and agreement on the product plan.

How do I reach you?

Email support@eplacestores.com or use the audit form below. You can also message me on X or Riselancer.
